Frequently Asked Questions

How is Kinetix different from standard workout trackers?

Most trackers are passive spreadsheets—you have to decide the weight, reps, and when to deload. Kinetix actively computes your fatigue and strength curve after every single set, adjusting your remaining workout in real time.

Do I need a smartwatch or wearable to use the app?

No. While syncing an Apple Watch, Garmin, or Whoop unlocks biometric rest timers and HRV readiness scores, our core progressive overload engine works seamlessly using your logged RPE and rep performance.

How does the Early Access Beta work?

We onboard new users in weekly cohorts to ensure server stability and gather high-signal feedback. Join the waitlist above, and you will receive an invite link via TestFlight (iOS) or Google Play Early Access.
